datawazi: professional workspace mockups (M13)

Three editors in the shape of Power BI Desktop: what you browse on the left, the work in the middle, its details on the right. You work directly, by dragging, right-clicking and typing. The AI appears inside each editor as a suggestion you accept or reject, and changes nothing behind your back. Data is the Uber trips example.

What's new

  • A real canvas: drag visuals to move them, handles to resize, with snap guides.
  • Build and Format panes to make a chart by hand: drag a field onto X-axis, Y-axis or Legend, which light up while you drag.
  • Slicers and filters on the page that every visual and Ask respect.

Where the AI went

  • Ctrl I opens a command bar on the canvas. Answers come from the data with "How this was calculated" and can be added as a chart.
  • "Build with AI" stays for a first draft, but every visual it makes is an ordinary one you edit.

For you to judge

  • Fixed page size (Power BI style), or a responsive grid that reflows on phones?
  • Command bar at the bottom, or a side panel you can pin open?
